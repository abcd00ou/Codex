How to Interpret Revenue-Leads-Capex

중요: revenue가 capex에 선행한다고 해서 “매출이 투자를 원인적으로 만든다”는 뜻은 아니다. 이 분석은 재무제표의 인식 시점 간 상관을 보는 것이므로, 아래와 같은 해석 후보로 읽어야 한다.

  • 납품/매출 인식 vs 자본화 시점 차이. 공급업체는 제품 출하나 진행률 기준으로 매출을 먼저 인식할 수 있고, 구매자는 설비 인도, 설치, 검수, 사용 가능 시점에 capex를 뒤늦게 자본화할 수 있다.
  • 주문-생산-검수 lag. 네트워킹, 메모리, 서버 부품 매출은 주문/출하 사이클을 빠르게 반영하지만, 하이퍼스케일러 capex는 데이터센터 build-out 예산 집행과 회계 처리 후 분기 재무제표에 잡힐 수 있다.
  • 공통 수요 shock의 다른 회계 표현. AI 수요 확대라는 동일한 shock이 공급업체에는 revenue로, 고객/플랫폼 사업자에는 capex로 나타나는 시점이 다를 수 있다.
  • 데이터/분류 한계. section aggregate는 회사 mix, fiscal quarter 차이, segment mix, 환율/통화 단위, capex 정의 차이를 포함한다. 따라서 reverse timing은 인과 결론이 아니라 “회계상 먼저 관측되는 지표” 후보로 보는 것이 맞다.

Methodology

Aggregation. Company financial rows are mapped to the user-defined supply-chain section. In DB mode, the section mapping comes from company_master.xlsx; in DataFrame mode, the input section column is used directly. Quarterly values are summed by section × calendar_quarter. Capex is converted to positive investment spend using absolute value because cash-flow statements can encode capex as an outflow.

Variable transformation. For capex and revenue/gross profit, the report uses year-over-year percentage change to reduce scale and seasonality effects: x_t / x_(t-4) - 1. For operating income and net income, the report uses year-over-year USD delta because profit can cross zero and make percentage growth unstable.

Lead-lag convention. lag +N means hyperscaler capex at quarter t is compared with target section metric at quarter t+N. lag 0 is same-quarter coupling. lag -N means the target section moved before hyperscaler capex.

Data-driven edge discovery. The data-driven map ignores the hypothesis list and evaluates every ordered section pair: source section capex change(t) → target section revenue YoY(t+lag). For each pair, it keeps the strongest positive correlation across -4Q to +8Q, then labels timing as source-capex-leads, synchronous, or target-revenue-leads. The map filters to n ≥ 8 and corr ≥ 0.45 for the primary picture.

Hypothesis edge method. The hypothesis-constrained chain-map section uses the directed hypothesis list supplied by the user. It reports the same lag convention, but it does not search outside those proposed edges. This makes it useful for checking whether a prior business narrative is supported, while the data-driven map is better for discovering unexpected timing.

Capex transform fallback. The primary source investment transform is capex_yoy_pct. If a source section has fewer than 8 capex YoY observations, the chain-edge analysis falls back to capex_qoq_pct. QoQ fallback edges are accepted with at least 4 overlapping observations, but if they remain below 8 observations they are classified as exploratory low-n. This is especially relevant for AI Chip, DRAM, and NAND capex, where available quarterly capex history is sparse in the current DB.

Signal classes. Forward evidence is classified from the best positive forward-lag correlation: strong ≥ 0.65, moderate ≥ 0.45, weak ≥ 0.25, very weak < 0.25, and no forward evidence when no positive forward-lag correlation has enough observations. These are analytical thresholds for exploration, not literature constants.
